Made for the computer you already have

SelvaraOS is a Debian desktop with its own shell. It runs well on new computers and on the old one in the drawer, and it sends nothing about you anywhere.

Selvara Search:
Find anything as you type

Like spotlight : find Apps, files, settings pages and quick sums in one field. Type three letters and Settings is already there.

Always one glance away

The Island at the top shows what is playing and lights up when the camera or microphone is on. The Control Center keeps the switches you use every day.

Selvara's dock

Badges for what is waiting, live window previews, and a volume for every app right from its icon.

Selvara Files:
Files without the clutter

A clean sidebar, real thumbnails and a split view when you need two folders at once.

Switch between light and dark in one click

Wallpapers come in light and dark pairs and switch with the theme. Windows that are already open follow along.

0

Zero connections on every build

Every build boots in a test machine that records its network traffic while it sits idle. The number of outgoing connections we expect is zero, and the build fails if it isn't.

No telemetry anywhereNo usage statistics, no crash uploads, no feedback prompts that phone home.

Nothing listeningNo service is open to the network. A firewall for incoming connections can be turned on in Settings.

AI only if you ask for itAnswers in Search are off by default, work only with your own key, and always say who receives your question.

Ready after a few choices

The first time you log in, Welcome asks how you like to work. Every answer applies at once and stays in Settings.
