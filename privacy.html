Your computer works for you

Privacy is the first rule every part of SelvaraOS follows. Here is what that means in practice.

No telemetry
SelvaraOS collects no usage data, no crash data and no identifiers. There is no account to create.
Nothing connects unless you ask
Checking for updates, setting the clock from the network and every other online feature is your choice, explained in plain words, and can be turned off.
Crash reports only if you send them
When an app crashes you can choose to report it. You read the whole report first, and it goes to our public issue tracker.
Your browser, your choice
The installer asks which browser you want and applies privacy-respecting settings.
AI is optional and open about it
Search can answer questions with AI only after you add your own key. It always names the provider that receives your question.
Free software you can inspect
Everything we write is public under the GPL-3.0 licence, so anyone can check these claims.

We test it, every time.

An automated test records all network traffic while a fresh system starts, logs in and sits idle for five minutes. A build that contacts any server fails.

Network Guard
A list of which app connected to which server, when, and how much data it used. One switch blocks any app.
App permissions
Apps ask before using your camera, microphone, screen, files or location. Change your mind at any time in Settings.
