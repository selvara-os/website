What's inside

An honest list. Each feature is marked as working in development builds, being built now, or planned.

  • 17 working
  • 4 in progress
  • 16 planned

Desktop

  • Top barSystem menu, the menus of the app you're using, status icons and the clock.
    Working
  • DockMagnification, live window previews, badges, and icons that bounce while an app starts.
    Working
  • App launcherFull screen or as a compact panel above the dock, with folders and search.
    Working
  • SearchApps, files, settings pages and calculations in one place.
    Working
  • Answers from AI in searchOff by default. Works only with your own key and always says who receives your question.
    Working
  • Control CenterWi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound, brightness, dark mode and media controls.
    Working
  • IslandMedia, volume and brightness, plus a live indicator when the microphone or camera is in use.
    Working
  • Clipboard historySearchable and stored only on your computer.
    Working
  • Selvara GlassTranslucent surfaces with adjustable strength and a Reduce transparency option.
    Working
  • Light, dark and accent colourSwitch in one click. Wallpapers and icons follow.
    Working
  • Rounded windowsSoft corners on every window.
    In progress
  • Dock stacks and drag and dropFolders that fan out, files dropped onto apps, apps dragged in to pin.
    Planned
  • Control Center you can rearrangeAdd, remove and reorder modules, or pin them to the top bar.
    Planned
  • Window snapping and several desktopsDrag windows to edges and corners, move between desktops with gestures.
    Planned
  • Focus modesWork, Personal, Sleep: choose which apps and people can reach you.
    Planned

Apps

  • TerminalFast and minimal, with an automatic fallback for older graphics.
    Working
  • SettingsOur own app with grouped pages and changes that apply at once.
    In progress
  • FilesInstant preview with the space bar, column and gallery views, a clean sidebar.
    Planned
  • StoreApps from Flathub and Debian in one list, with clear permissions.
    Planned
  • Preview, Text Editor, Notes, Reminders, CalendarSimple everyday apps that keep your data on your computer.
    Planned
  • Keychain and BackupSee your saved passwords. Automatic, encrypted backups you can browse by date.
    Planned

Privacy and security

  • No telemetry, testedNetwork traffic is recorded on every build. The expected number of outgoing connections is zero.
    Working
  • Firewall and app confinementIncoming connections are blocked and no service is open to the network.
    Working
  • Network GuardSee which app connects where, and block any app with one switch.
    Planned
  • App permissionsCamera, microphone, screen, files and location: asked the first time, listed per app.
    Planned
  • Disk encryptionOffered when you install.
    Planned

System

  • Silent start-upA clean splash from power-on to login, with no scrolling text.
    Working
  • Start-up menuHidden until you need it, in the same design as the rest of the system.
    Working
  • Protection when memory runs lowCompressed memory and safeguards against freezes on 4 GB machines.
    Working
  • AccessibilityScreen reader, zoom, reduced motion and transparency, sticky keys.
    Working
  • InstallerInstall from a USB stick, on its own or next to your current system.
    In progress
  • LanguagesEnglish and Italian today. French, German, Spanish and Dutch next.
    In progress
  • Snapshots and roll backReturn to the state before an update from the start-up menu.
    Planned
  • Updates without interruptionsNever a forced restart. You choose when.
    Planned
  • System driversGraphics and Wi-Fi drivers offered with a clear explanation.
    Planned
  • Drop and phone linkSend files to nearby devices, compatible with LocalSend, and see phone notifications on your desktop.
    Planned
  • Migration assistantBring files and bookmarks from your old computer.
    Planned
